# References: Federalism and Federal Powers

1. [Federalism in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federalism_in_the_United_States) - Wikipedia - Thorough overview of American federalism's historical evolution from dual federalism through cooperative and competitive models, with analysis of landmark Supreme Court decisions shaping the federal-state balance.

2. [McCulloch v. Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McCulloch_v._Maryland) - Wikipedia - Analysis of the 1819 landmark case establishing the Necessary and Proper Clause's broad scope and the doctrine of national supremacy — the constitutional foundation of modern federal power.

3. [Commerce Clause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commerce_Clause) - Wikipedia - Covers the constitutional text, historical development, and major cases (Gibbons v. Ogden, Wickard v. Filburn, Lopez, Morrison) that define Congress's most expansive source of regulatory authority.

4. American Government: Power and Purpose (15th Edition) - Lowi, Ginsberg, Shepsle, and Ansolabehere - W.W. Norton - Chapter 3 examines the constitutional allocation of powers between national and state governments, grant-in-aid programs, and the ongoing federalism debates in Congress and the courts.

5. Keeping the Republic: Power and Citizenship in American Politics (8th Edition) - Barbour and Wright - CQ Press - Provides student-accessible explanations of dual, cooperative, and competitive federalism with contemporary policy examples including healthcare, immigration, and marijuana legalization.

6. [Federalism — Khan Academy AP US Government](https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/ap-government-and-civics/foundations-of-american-democracy/federalism/a/federalism-in-the-united-states) - Khan Academy - Free video lessons and articles covering categorical grants, block grants, unfunded mandates, and the anti-commandeering doctrine, aligned to AP exam learning targets.

7. [Federalism: Crash Course Government and Politics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J0gosGXddcg) - CrashCourse / YouTube - Engaging 10-minute video clearly explaining marble-cake vs. layer-cake federalism and why the balance of power has shifted toward Washington since the New Deal era.

8. [Federalism and the States](https://ballotpedia.org/Federalism) - Ballotpedia - Up-to-date, nonpartisan coverage of current federalism disputes, including state challenges to federal regulations and the latest Supreme Court rulings on preemption and the Spending Clause.

9. [Necessary and Proper Clause](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution-conan/article-1/section-8/clause-18) - Cornell LII - Annotated clause-by-clause analysis drawing on every Supreme Court case interpreting the elastic clause, with links to full opinions and scholarly commentary.

10. [NCSL — Federal and State Authority Reference Guide](https://www.ncsl.org/research/about-state-legislatures/preemption.aspx) - National Conference of State Legislatures - Practical overview of federal preemption, block grants, and current state-federal conflicts written for policymakers, providing real-world examples of cooperative federalism in action.
