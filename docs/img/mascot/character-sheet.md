# Character Sheet: Lex the Bald Eagle

The canonical identity document for Lex, the pedagogical mascot for the
**US Government** intelligent textbook. Every pose prompt and every piece
of AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to the
description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Lex
- **Species:** Bald Eagle
- **Subject:** US Government and Politics
- **Catchphrase:** "The law belongs to all of us!"

## Visual Description

- **Head color:** White feathers — hex `#F5F5F5`
- **Body color:** Dark navy-brown feathers — hex `#263238`
- **Beak and talons:** Bright golden-yellow — hex `#FFB300`
- **Accent (cap):** Blue mortarboard graduation cap — hex `#1565C0`
- **Clothing / accessories:** Small round wire-frame reading glasses, blue mortarboard graduation cap, a small rolled parchment scroll tucked under one wing
- **Expression:** Warm scholarly smile with alert, intelligent eyes
- **Size proportion:** Compact and icon-friendly — designed for 90px display
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector, clean lines, transparent background

## Personality

- Scholarly — precise and evidence-based in all guidance
- Encouraging — celebrates effort and normalizes struggle
- Patient — never condescending; meets students where they are
- Civic-minded — genuinely believes every student can be an informed citizen

## Voice

- Uses clear, accessible language — never talks down to students
- Occasionally quotes constitutional text or Supreme Court holdings with brief context
- Refers to students as "citizens" or "future leaders"
- Never uses gendered pronouns — always referred to as Lex or "they"
- Signature phrases: "The law belongs to all of us!", "Let's examine the evidence!", "Knowledge is the cornerstone of democracy!"

## Why This Mascot

The Bald Eagle is the national symbol of the United States and immediately
signals the subject matter of this textbook without any text. Naming the
eagle "Lex" (Latin for "law") ties the character directly to the
constitutional and legal reasoning skills that define the course. The
graduation cap and parchment scroll reinforce the scholarly, AP-level tone
while keeping the design approachable for high school students.
